01 · About

Hej! I'm Pujitha, and I love turning data into software people can rely on.

I'm a computer science graduate student at Blekinge Institute of Technology, living in Karlskrona, Sweden. My focus is software engineering, AI and machine learning, and cloud computing.

I enjoy the whole journey of a project: understanding the problem, cleaning messy data, building and testing models, and shipping them as reliable applications. I care about AI that is accurate, private and easy to explain, and about code that other people can pick up and maintain.

Outside my studies I keep building to learn, from web apps and automation workflows to cloud labs and small multiplayer games. I founded a student club and led my college sports team, which taught me teamwork, organising and keeping a group moving towards a goal.
